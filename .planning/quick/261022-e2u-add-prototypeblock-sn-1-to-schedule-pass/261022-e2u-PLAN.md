Add the PrototypeBlock (SN = -1) to the schedule pass (user ruling 2026-10-22, decisions D-1..D-5).
MemNew (GMEM — launcher-provided buffer, schedule.md §4.2) and InputParam (positional parameter)
nodes are placeholders to known states, NOT statements: they are anchored at SN -1 into a
kernel-signature block rendered FIRST. This makes the SN→lexical-scope mapping TOTAL (-1 =
signature scope, 0 = kernel scope, r ≥ 1 = depth-r loop) with zero special conditions in the
algorithm — because propagation is a max, a -1 dep contributes nothing.

Purpose: the compiled-kernel shape gains its signature scope as a first-class scheduled view;
final_lower will consume the prototype block as the canonical SCHEDULE view of params + GMEM
placeholders, while Params []Node / Gmem []ASTGmemAlloc stay as structured metadata views (D-5).
Output: amended spec, extended IR (BlockPrototype/PrototypeSN/ASTKernel.Prototype), updated
