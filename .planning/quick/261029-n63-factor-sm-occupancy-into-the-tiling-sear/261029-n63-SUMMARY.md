# Quick 261029-n63: Factor SM Occupancy into the Tiling Search Objective

**The tiling search now selects by score' = traffic × (1 − idleFrac) × (ε + (1−ε)·fill) with occupancyOf as the single shared source — the GPU suite re-validated 14/14 with the six compute kernels 2.7×–29× faster (mm_small 7.92ms → 2.91ms) and zero regressions beyond ±1.2% noise.**

## What Was Built

- `occupancyOf` / `occupancyCompute` (`internal/opt/tiling/constraints.go`): the
  single source of the occupancy arithmetic — `grid = Π LoC_d`,
  `activeGrid = Π min(LoC_d, ceil(NominalExtent/T_d))` (uniform stand-in
  extent per CTA dim), `blockDim` via `blockDimOf` (judge.go — no
  re-derivation), floor-divided `ctasPerSM` (warp/threads/smem terms, smem
  term clamped to 0 when the window cannot fit), `capacity = SMs ×
  ctasPerSM`, `idleFrac = 1 − activeGrid/grid`, `fill = min(1,
  activeGrid/capacity)`, `factor = (1−idleFrac) × (ε + (1−ε)·fill)`. All
  clamps total — `capacity == 0` degrades to the ε floor, never a
  divide-by-zero.
- `Params` (`internal/opt/tiling/record.go`): six A6000/sm_86-pinned fields
  — `SMs=84, WarpsPerSM=48, ThreadsPerSM=1536, SmemPerSM=102400,
  ReservedSmemPerCTA=1024, OccEps=0.15` — with Validate rejecting
  non-positive counts and `OccEps` outside `(0,1]`; `NominalExtent`'s doc
  now names its `activeGrid` role.
- `search.go`: `schedResult.score` is `float64` with a sibling raw
  `traffic int` (fixed-schedule traffic pins survive); `evalSchedule`
  applies the factor AFTER the greedy eviction loop on the post-eviction
  promoted bytes; `better()` shape, enumeration order, partism tiebreak,
  and first-found tie resolution unchanged; determinism comment updated
  to name `(score', partitionCount)`.
- `occupancy_test.go` (new, ~230 lines): unit pins of the locked mm_small
  math (LoC=64/tile=256 factor ≈ 0.07798; LoC=16 ≈ 0.31190; exactly 4× at
  equal traffic), the capacity==0 clamp, the multi-dim product, the
  shared-wrapper contract (`occupancyOf` ≡ `occupancyCompute`, blockDim
  from `blockDimOf`), and the six Params defaults + Validate failures.
- Test re-pins: `TestTrafficArithmeticAndOptimality` + `enumerateBest`
  both compute score' via the SHARED `occupancyOf` and compare float64;
  `TestParamsSurface`/`TestDefaultParams`/`TestParamsValidate` cover the
  six new fields; the e2e `fePinScheduleSignature` re-pinned from the
  freshly emitted .cu with the full score' derivation in the comment.

## Deviations from Plan

None against the locked design. Notes:

1. **e2e re-chosen vector differs from the plan's illustrative one (by
   design).** The plan's headline unit math (LoC=16 beats LoC=64 by ~4× at
   EQUAL traffic) is pinned exactly in `occupancy_test.go`. On the real
   frontend fixture the winner became `M=CTA(T=32,LoC=64) ×
   N(reduced)=WARP/32` (grid fully active at T=32: idleFrac 0, factor
   ≈0.7976, traffic 8355840 → score' ≈6.66M) over the window-carrying
   LoC=16/T=256 vector (idleFrac 0.75, factor ≈0.3119, traffic 16776960 →
   score' ≈5.23M). Re-derived from the emitted .cu per the plan's own "re-
   derive, don't assume" rule; the pin comment carries the derivation.
2. **Pre-landed test bug (mine, caught before commit).** The multi-dim pin's
   hand-computed expectations were wrong twice (fill = 128/924 not 1; the
   clamp case's grid was too small to reach capacity) — fixed the TEST, not
   the implementation; the final pins match the locked formula.
3. No CG golden regeneration needed — no `.cu` testdata drifted (cg
   emit-test fixtures are hand-built IR, independent of the search).

**Attribution (before/after `.cu` diff per moved kernel):**

- **Family A — idle-grid shrink (LoC 64→16 at T=256):** `scale_copy`,
  `softmax`, `transpose`, `row_sum`, `layernorm`. The old grid of 64 CTAs
  had only 16 active at the nominal extent (idleFrac 0.75); the new grid
  is exactly 16 CTAs (idleFrac 0). Memory-bound kernels: timings within
  ±1.2% noise. Correctness re-verified by the suite run above.
- **Family B — idle-free grid + full-warp split-k (the big wins):**
  `mm_small`, `mm_mid`, `mm_splitk`, `gemm`, `qk_score`, `mlp`. The
  search kept grid 64 but dropped the CTA tile to 32 (activeGrid 64 =
  grid, idleFrac 0) and demoted the 64 KB rhs SMEM window in favor of a
  32 B/thread FRAG window carried by 32 warps (blockDim 1024, smem pool
  128 B reducer staging only). Per-CTA parallelism went 2 warps → 32
  warps; the occupancy factor (0.7976 vs the window vector's 0.3119)
  legitimately re-ranked these vectors. All six are the intended
  occupancy re-choice; numerics are suite-verified (maxdiff 2.9e-6…5.3e-5,
  unchanged tolerances).

No kernel regressed beyond noise; no pin was weakened (the only decision
pin that moved — the e2e split-k signature — was re-derived, and the
winner is legal by the unchanged MAPM/MALoC/CuMAP/MASRAM constraints,
which tiling-verify re-checks on every run).
