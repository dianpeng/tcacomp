price multi-element windows (the current PI T_d x dtype vs MaxFragPerThread
decline is what zeroes every full-collapse vector)." This task lands exactly
that pricing slice — NOTHING in codegen/lowering: the schedule may select the
all-SEQ vector, and whether the emitted CUDA is legal/fast is verified by the
e2e suite (memcheck is part of the protocol). Register blocking in codegen is
the follow-up fix6 work, explicitly out of scope.
