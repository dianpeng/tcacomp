## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 — Bug] tiling-verify's kernel-wide FRAG sum re-priced under the verify pass's own budget**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (GREEN), exposed by TestVerifyKernelWideFragGate (expected green unchanged, went red)
- **Issue:** verify.go summed `j.bytes` from the judge re-run under THIS pass's `MaxFragPerThread`. Post-pricing `j.bytes = min(raw, budget)` is budget-dependent, so under the pin's deliberate 31-byte budget the record's 32 B block re-priced to 31 B, the sum never exceeded the budget, and the kernel-wide gate could never fire — the 31-byte pin instead spuriously fired the record-vs-judge cross-check ("record (…bytes 32) disagrees with the judge (…bytes 31)").
- **Fix:** the kernel-wide sums now account the RECORD's footprints (`sd.Bytes` — what the lower's budget resolution will actually consume), gated against this pass's thresholds; the judge cross-check (mirrored-params contract, already documented) runs only after the gate passes. SMEM behavior identical (`sd.Bytes == j.bytes` for SMEM by construction).
- **Files modified:** internal/opt/tiling/verify.go
- **Commit:** 010ba91
